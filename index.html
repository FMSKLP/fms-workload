Not sure? Use the complexity calculator

Rate three things about this case. The weighted score picks the complexity for you.

Score = 40% clinical + 30% coordination + 30% risk. Below 1.5 is simple (×0.5), 1.5 to 2.4 is less complex (×1), 2.5 to 3.9 is moderately complex (×1.5), 4.0 and above is very complex (×2).

      Backup and transfer

      Your log lives only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases it, so copy a backup regularly. Paste a backup on another device to move your log there.

      Restore replaces everything on this device with the backup. Merge adds the backup's entries to what's already here, skips duplicates, and keeps this device's name, KK and targets.
